Durable copy

Uploads sit in quarantine and are deleted 30 days after upload. Promotion copies the archive into private durable storage. The copy is accepted only if its bytes hash to the SHA-256 below and its size matches. The quarantine original is left untouched. Safe to retry.

What happens next

Upload completion confirms the received parts and total size. It does not verify the competition schema or measurements. A bounded local inspector checks archive safety and records file hashes before research use. External AI access and automated extraction remain unconfigured.
