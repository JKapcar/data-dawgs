Checking your account.

Applies to every assistant or other client holding your personal connector credential. It does not verify which assistant sent a request. Turning an app off leaves Bozo and the other app unchanged.

One connection, shared work

  1. Approve an app below for seven days. Neither app is enabled by default.
  2. In each personal assistant that supports your existing DataDawgs connector, refresh its tools. Keep the existing URL; do not mint a replacement just to add these apps.
  3. Ask it to read the workspace, then make a specific contribution. The changes appear in Pound or BDB.

New assistants need their own supported connector setup. Claude, Gemini, Grok and Muse have not been verified on your accounts here. This page does not connect providers or run a daily schedule.

No raw competition downloads, held-out execution, experiment runs, roster edits or permission changes are available through these tools. Private text you put in the workspace is shared when read.
