NFL Weather. Pup

Kickoff-window forecasts from the National Weather Service at stadium coordinates. Roof class is a registry we maintain. This is not a weather-impact model and not somebody else’s notes.

No oracle lives here. NWS is a forecast dated by updateTime. Fixed roofs ignore outdoor numbers for play. Retractable roofs may be closed; we do not know. Flag chips are thresholds, not evidence that scoring changes. Nothing on this page has been graded.

Method

What is measured, what is assumed, what would promote this off Pup.

Inputs: locked schedule, stadium registry with lat/lon and roof class, and the NWS hourly grid. The page does not store forecasts.

Flags fire at ≥15 mph wind, ≥40% precip, ≤32°F, or ≥90°F. Cutoffs are readable, not graded against scoring.

Kevin Roth’s MySportsWeather is interpretation. This page is the raw official grid.
