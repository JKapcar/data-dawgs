How this works — and what it can’t tell you

Rosters are frozen at the time you load them. Sleeper projections are third-party, unaudited inputs. Injuries, trades, waivers, lineup choices, and correlation between teams are not modeled. Before three completed weeks, σ is a generic stated assumption rather than a league estimate.
