Open decision · publication scope

Every other machine surface on this site is public sports data. This one is bodyweight, girths, HRV, sleep and progress photos for one identifiable person. The doctrine that makes the rest of the site work does not transfer automatically. Decide scope before real logging accumulates — after that it has been crawled.
