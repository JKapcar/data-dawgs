House rules

Every number on this site is a dated snapshot, and it says so. Structural claims (positional scoring curves, dead-zone shape) are separated from one analyst's player takes. Strong claims get an outside-view check against base rates before they're believed. Point estimates are treated as ranges. And when we're advocating for a player we already like, we try to catch ourselves selecting the evidence.
