How the grading works

The short version of what every number on this page is, and what it is not.

The field. Opponent lineups are sampled from your ownership projection with the same legal Showdown sampler DFS Labs uses, calibrated toward your captain and FLEX ownership. Each contest type tilts that one field by a sharpness preset: max-entry fields lean toward higher projection, the flagship leans casual. The presets are hypotheses until your own contest standings calibrate them.

The bands. At each ownership (or salary, or dupe-risk) level, the shaded bands are the field's 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentile of median or ceiling points. Below the 25th is Weak, to the 75th Typical, to the 90th Strong, above it Elite. The dashed line is the best legal lineup available at that level. A band is a description of the field, not a win chance.

The worlds. Scores come from the DFS Labs engine's correlated outcome model: one draw per game over correlations measured on 2019–2025 nflverse data, with your projections as the means. Finish odds, cash and top-1% rates are counted against the contest type's field in every world.

The grade. A weighted blend of four percentiles against that field: points for the ownership, ceiling, top-1% chance, and cash chance (uniqueness replaces cash in max-entry and flagship contests). The weights are a placeholder until receipts can grade them, and the page shows them in Pro.
