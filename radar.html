Read this before trusting the shape. Waist feeds three of the eight spokes — it is its own axis, it drives the Navy body-fat figure, and it is the denominator of the V-taper ratio. Waist progress therefore inflates apparent overall convergence by roughly a factor of three. The four independent spokes are chest, flexed arm, shoulders, and weight. When the polygon looks lopsided toward the right side of the dial, that is the double-counting, not a training result.
