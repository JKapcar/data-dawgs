What a receipt records

FieldWhy it is there
ClaimA statement that can only resolve one way.
Probability rangeA range, not a point — 60–70% says something a bare 65% hides about how sure you are of the estimate itself.
Base rateThe outside view, written down before the case-specific adjustment.
What would change my mindNamed in advance, so an update is a plan rather than a rationalization.
Resolve-by dateNo open-ended calls. Undated predictions never fail.
Criteria + fine printSource-named and locked at creation. Edge cases go in the fine print, not in an argument afterwards.
BenchmarkThe thing being beaten, declared up front — per domain, before the first graded call in that domain.
ResolutionYes / No / Ambiguous, plus a note on what was actually learned.

What this can't do

This is a self-reported record, and its only real defence against highlight-reel bias is procedural: every prediction is logged when it is made, with a resolve-by date and locked criteria, and none are ever deleted. That is a weaker guarantee than an exchange with money on it — take it as such. Calibration needs volume, and early buckets are noise; watch the per-bucket counts before reading anything into the shape of the curve. Padding the log with easy high-confidence calls flatters calibration without demonstrating any skill, which is exactly why hard calls are tagged and scored against a declared baseline rather than counted as wins. A good Brier score on trivial questions means very little.
