FAAB War Plan Framework · editorial · 2026-08-26

Two structural facts drive guillotine FAAB: supply rises every week (each chop dumps a full roster, eventually stars, into the pool) while the money dies with the chopped (unspent budget leaves the market). Talent gets cheaper all season; the only reason to pay early prices is that surviving September is worth the premium. Your branch depends on your margin over the chop line, not the standings.

Strong team
Clearing the line by 20+ — spend under 10% before ~Week 6 unless a top-15 player chops, strike in Weeks 8–12 for a title anchor, keep a reserve to outbid-block late (remaining budgets are visible to everyone)
Weak team
Living within ~10 of the line — one transformative 20–30% bid in Weeks 2–4 on the best chopped player beats two medium adds; dead teams don’t get to hoard

How this works The seven modules, the maths behind the ladder, and an honest list of what this tool cannot do.

How survival compounds Illustration — not your league

Six weeks of 90% survival is not 90%. It is 53%. That compounding is the whole reason a weekly “On the Block” flag is not enough.

Modules

01

Sleeper sync Observed

Paste a league ID. Sleeper's read-only API needs no login and no token, so there is nothing to authorize and nothing that can write to your league.

02

Chop-line projector Modeled

The modeled weekly minimum across surviving teams, and your margin above or below it. Quoted as a median with an 80% band, because a single number oversells what a simulation knows.

03

Survival odds Modeled

Monte Carlo over each team's completed-score baseline for one weekly stage. It does not ingest current in-game scores.

04

FAAB pacing Observed

Dollars appreciate as the pool shrinks: ~$18,000 across 18 teams in week 1 is nearly worthless; ~$400 across five teams in December is not. Reads every team's remaining budget from your league.

05

Floor-first lineup Planned

Maximizes P(score > chop line) rather than expected points. Gated on a per-player weekly projection source with variance — a league's own history is too thin per player to fake one, so this waits rather than pretends.

06

Waiver value Observed

Every elimination dumps a full roster into the pool. Who is unrostered in your league and what they actually scored in your scoring — observed points, not projections.

07

Draft War Room Observed picks

Reads a Sleeper draft read-only and strikes the Guillotine Board as picks land. Set your slot and the snake math counts picks until you’re up. The board itself is a dated editorial re-rank over the site’s MV snapshot — opinion, not projection.

What this can't do

Survival odds are a simulation from completed-score history and its variance — they assume the next week plays like a typical past week. Before two completed weeks exist the page runs in projection mode instead: Sleeper season player projections build each roster’s best projected lineup and every team carries an assumed ±21-point spread — byes, injuries and schedule are not modeled there, and nothing in that mode is observed. They cannot see live Sunday scores, an inactive, or a garbage-time spike. Prediction receipts are local-device only and are not immutable, server-persisted, cross-device or graded. Projection-driven FAAB bid recommendations, full conditional waiver optimization, Universal Data Vault, and injury, bye-week, positional-depth and player-concentration fragility components are all Planned. The Long Game’s season Monte Carlo rolls the weekly model forward with rosters frozen at their current mean and spread — it cannot see waivers, injuries, byes, or the talent redistribution every chop causes, those omissions compound with every simulated week, and its finishing places cover the simulated teams only. The observed FAAB module encodes a framework about dollar appreciation, not a guarantee. The Draft War Room polls Sleeper every few seconds — near-live, not instant — and never writes to Sleeper; its pick matching is a name-and-position heuristic that can miss an unusual name, which a tap on the row fixes. The Guillotine Board’s ranks, tiers and flags are editorial opinion built 2026-08-26 over the 2026-08-24 MV snapshot, not projections, and the FAAB War Plan is a dated editorial framework, not a bid engine.
