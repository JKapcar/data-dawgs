Who's in this league

The roster is the size — the ticket locks when everyone here has a leg in. Join links are stored hashed, so a lost one can't be looked up, only re-issued.

Join link

One link, reusable, safe to drop in the group chat. It does not create accounts and it does not carry a password — people sign up under their own name and the link puts them in this league. Rotate it and the old one dies instantly. The cap is your ceiling: a leaked link can never grow the league past it.
